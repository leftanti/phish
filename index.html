How to run Phish Fortunes

  1. Open this board, maximise it and share this window on your call (tick include sound).
  2. Click Host controls. A second window opens with every answer, a speaker note per step, strikes, bonus and honourable mentions. Keep it off-screen.
  3. Ask the room: a user reports a phishing email. What do you do? Reveal steps as people shout them out, in any order.
  4. Wrong answers get a strike. There are three slots, but nobody said strikes stop at three.
  5. Good answers that aren't on the board go into honourable mentions.

No host window? Press H for a drawer, or type a step number to flip it, X strike, Z undo, B bonus. Progress saves in your browser and Reset game clears it.
